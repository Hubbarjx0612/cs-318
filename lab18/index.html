Harry Potter Books and Movies

The Harry Potter series was written by British author J.K. Rowling. She wrote the seven-book saga between 1997 and 2007, which became one of the best-selling series in history.

Chris Columbus directed the first two Harry Potter films, Harry Potter and the Sorcerer's Stone (2001) and Harry Potter and the Chamber of Secrets (2002), launching the franchise. Producer David Heyman initiated the series by securing the film rights from J.K. Rowling in 1999, which were then produced by Warner Bros. Pictures.

Harry Potter

A boy who learns on his eleventh birthday that he is the orphaned son of two powerful wizards and possesses unique magical powers of his own. He is summoned from his life as an unwanted child to become a student at Hogwarts, an English boarding school for wizards. There, he meets several friends who become his closest friends and help him discover the truth about his parents' mysterious deaths.
